photonic diffractometer

parts

laser diode (DPSS preferred) - options: 650nm red
1x/2x aspheric lens to collimate the beam
>= 1x photodiode (can use one or build a sensor fusion array)
2x stepper motor with microstep driver resolution
goniometer to measure angle (optional)
ads1115 adc units (for refining and amplifying analog photodiode signals) - optimize SNR (use ads1x15 arduino library)
wires, headers, crimp wire jumper pins (maybe buy the kit set)
(optional) 2x polarizers to study TE/TM modes

3d printed parts

sample stage
diffractometer base mount
stepper motor arms
laser diode & photodiode array casing

theory

bragg's law

purpose

- to measure diffraction efficiency of metasurface gratings
- to verify periodicity and lattice spacing
- to characterize angular scattering and coupling (key to beam steering & fourier-like operations)
- to measure coupling efficiency
- run diagnostics to determine if a metasurface will function as an optical computing element

usage

measure lattice spacing, periodicity - yes, can measure within range
coupling in photonic structures - partially measurable (requires sweep and polarization control)
photonic crystals/photonic crystal resonators - partially measurable (near 650 nm if laser is same)
colloidal crystals - yes, can measure
metasurfaces/metamaterials - partially measurable (need polarization control for TE/TM)
zero-index mediums
optical computing (oc) materials
waveguides - mostly not measurable
bandgap devices - partially measurable

sweet spot analysis (best results)

colloidal crystals/opals (PMMA, polystyrene beads, silica spheres)
  - act as 3D photonic crystals that create photonic bandgaps
  - low-loss interconnects, cavity resonators, and optical filters in computing chips
1D/2D photonic crystals (etched gratings, periodic nanopatterns)
  - provide waveguides & cavities with high Q-factor (core logic & memory of photonic chips)
  - enable slow-light effects for delay lines and synchronization
  - basis for all-optical switches, multiplexers, and demultiplexers in photonic processors
surface-relief gratings (diffraction gratings, DOEs, holographic gratings)
  - used as couplers to inject/extract light into integrated waveguides
  - enable beam steering & wavelength routing (optical interconnect fabrics)
  - form foundation of fourier-optical computing (where computing is done in the spatial-frequency domain)
plasma-deposited multilayers/Bragg mirrors (SiO₂/TiO₂, Si₃N₄ stacks)
  - act as optical mirrors/reflectors for resonators & vertical-cavity lasers
  - used to confine photons in optical memory / cavity-based logic gates
  - enable filters & band-selective routing of signals in computing chips
metasurface gratings - periodic nanoantennas, nanopost arrays (periodic dielectric/metallic nanostructures tuned to red light)
  - provide beam steering, wavefront shaping, and phase control at the chip level
  - serve as optical interconnects and multiplexers
  - enables flat optics (compact optical logic circuits and AI photonic accelerators)
  - can implement matrix-vector multiplication in optical domain (metasurface computing)

moderate results

photonic crystal resonators/bandgap devices
metasurfaces with polarization-dependent responses
plasmonic arrays (Au, Ag nanoparticle lattices, nanohole arrays)
phase-change metasurfaces (GST, Sb₂Se₃)
transparent conducting oxides (ITO, AZO films patterned into gratings)
